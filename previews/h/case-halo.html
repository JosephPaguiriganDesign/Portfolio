Designed for a controller, at ten feet.

Console-first and controller-driven: every menu had to read instantly and navigate cleanly on a gamepad, from the couch. In a game, a clumsy menu isn't a papercut — it's the first thing a player feels, every single session. Tight constraints, high stakes, zero room for confusion.
