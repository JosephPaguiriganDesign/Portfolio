Art direction previews

Six directions for Joseph Paguirigan’s portfolio. Same copy, same case studies. A–C are the first three systems. D–F are the radical typographic set. The live site is unchanged.

  1. A · Expressive editorialCropped condensed type, one printer’s red, proof marks on the headers only. The work sits in the first screen.
  2. B · Swiss indexA strict grid and a project index with role, year and outcome. Built to scan like a design system.
  3. C · Design engineerCase studies as product components, spec notes, and a light/dark toggle. It looks shipped.
  4. D · ProofThe site is a marked-up press proof. A struck “Comps.” and ballpoint corrections, then the case studies as galleys.
  5. E · Broken / rebuiltDE / SIGN / ER collide and overprint, then the case list locks into a system.
  6. F · SignalItalic serif drifts like a wave. A condensed line cuts through, and the case titles step out of it.
