01 / Status taxonomy

Six states, globally consistent.

The old system had 27 status strings across regions. We collapsed them to six clear states — In progress, Awaiting consent, Needs review, Flagged, Complete, Cancelled — with a single color language that survives translation.

02 / Real-time spine

Updated, not refreshed.

Behind the dashboard, a websocket bus pushes status changes within seconds of upstream vendors completing a check. Recruiters stop refreshing; the table just updates.

03 / Filters & saved views

Built for power users.

Every recruiter has a "Mine, urgent" view. Operations has "Stalled > 5 days." Legal has "Flagged, North America." Filters compose into saved URLs they paste into Slack.

02  ·  The Adjudication Surface

Compliance-dense.
Decision-ready.

Candidate Report

A background report is one of the densest documents in HR — criminal history, education, identity, credit, drug testing, motor-vehicle records. The redesign organizes it so a hiring manager can read it in two minutes, and adjudicators can act in five.

04 / Hierarchy

Decision first, data underneath.

A red "Needs review" headline pill, the count of clear/flagged checks, then the dense detail. Hiring managers scan; adjudicators read; everyone gets what they need.

05 / Timeline

Every event, timestamped.

Right-side activity feed shows the order's full life — consent, vendor calls, returns, escalations. The same audit log that compliance officers used to dig out of database exports.

06 / Recommendation

Rules transparent, never hidden.

When the rules engine recommends "Engage," it shows its work — which policy, what thresholds. Adjudicators retain full FCRA-compliant override authority.
